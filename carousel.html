TOP 5 beach destinations for 2026

Grace Bay in Turks and Caicos offers calm, turquoise waters and powdery white sand protected by a beautiful barrier reef. Brazil’s Baia do Sancho features dramatic cliffs, emerald waters, and pristine snorkeling spots. Sanya on Hainan Island combines miles of golden coastline with an incredible local seafood scene. Ipanema Beach in Rio de Janeiro delivers a vibrant city atmosphere, culture, and stunning mountain backdrops. Finally, The Pass in Byron Bay is a legendary surf break boasting soft sand and spectacular coastal trails. These five great global destinations represent the absolute pinnacle of coastal beauty, diverse activities, and breathtaking tropical landscapes.

Here are highly rated hotel recommendations for two of the top beach destinations—Grace Bay (Turks and Caicos) and Ipanema Beach (Rio de Janeiro, Brazil) :

  1. Seven Stars Resort & Spa: A gorgeous 5-star resort right on the sand with a heated saltwater pool, three restaurants, and spacious balconies offering spectacular views. Price: ~776 €/night.
  2. Wymara Resort (formerly Gansevoort): Known for its sleek, modern design, world-class amenities, and an unbeatable location right on Grace Bay Beach. Price: ~741 €/night.
  3. Hotel Arpoador: Situated right at the base of the Arpoador peninsula. It features a chic, minimalist Brazilian design, rooftop yoga with mountain views, and direct beach access between Ipanema and Copacabana. Price: ~293 €/night.
  4. Ipanema Inn: Located just one block away from the beach, this cozy hotel features a highly-rated restaurant and includes complimentary beach service with umbrellas for guests. Price: ~174 €/night.
  5. Janeiro Hotel: A luxury, eco-chic boutique property in neighboring Leblon offering breathtaking views over the bay and an authentic "Carioca" lifestyle design. Price: ~434 €/night.

Pro Tipps

  1. Best Snorkeling Spot: Walk all the way down to Coral Gardens (Bight Reef). You can swim right off the beach into a protected reef filled with sea turtles and stingrays without paying for a boat tour.
  2. Avoiding Crowds: Visit during the shoulder season (April to May) for lower resort prices and empty stretches of sand.

Grace Bay provides a pristine paradise of impossibly clear turquoise waters and calm, powdery white sands protected by a massive barrier reef. Baia do Sancho delivers untamed, dramatic romance with its hidden emerald bays, towering volcanic cliffs, and world-class snorkeling. In Sanya, you experience a unique fusion of expansive golden tropical coastlines and vibrant, authentic local culinary night markets. Ipanema Beach immerses you in a high-energy cultural hotspot, where stunning mountain peaks meet an iconic, rhythmic city vibe. Finally, The Pass captures the ultimate laid-back coastal lifestyle, blending legendary surf breaks with breathtaking, panoramic cliffside walking trails.
